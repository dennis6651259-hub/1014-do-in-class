📊 Core Empirical Findings & Benchmark Matrix

🎯 1. The 2-Feature Sweet Spot (k = 2)

Across all 5 feature selection algorithms, models converge on ['R&D Spend', 'Marketing Spend'] as the optimal subset. Minimum Test RMSE drops to $8,198.80 with peak Test R² of 0.9474. R&D spend alone accounts for 94.65% of variation, and Marketing adds complementary positive lift.

⚠️ 2. The 5-Feature Overfitting Cliff (k = 5)

Adding redundant variables (Administration and state location dummies) introduces multicollinearity. Test RMSE spikes from $8,198.80 to $9,137.99 (a +$939 penalty), and R² degrades to 0.9347. Parsimony (Occam's Razor) decisively favors the 2-variable model.
